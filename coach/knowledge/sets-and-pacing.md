# Sets and pacing

## Splitting the daily goal
The daily goal does not have to be done in one go. Splitting it into sets with 30 to 90 seconds of rest between them keeps form better and makes high-volume days manageable. For example, 80 squats can be four sets of 20. Sets can also be spread across the day, and all of them count towards the day.

## When a set falls apart
When reps start getting shallow or fast, that set is done. Stop, rest, and start a fresh set. Ten good reps help more than ten poor ones.

## Missing the target
Falling short of the target on a hard day is normal, especially in week 2 and week 4 when volume climbs. Finish the day with smaller sets rather than skipping the rest of it. Repeated misses mean the jump was too big; the week 3 deload is there to absorb that.

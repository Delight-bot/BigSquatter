# Recovery, rest days and streaks

## Rest days
Every seventh day of the challenge is a rest day. Muscles adapt during recovery, not during the workout itself, so rest days are part of the plan rather than a break from it. Light walking or mobility work is fine.

## Muscle soreness
Soreness one to two days after a big jump in volume is normal and usually fades within a few days. Gentle movement, sleep, and drinking enough water help. Doing a lighter set while sore is generally fine; soreness alone is not a reason to stop the challenge.

## Missed days and broken streaks
A missed day breaks the streak but not the challenge. The best response is to do the current day's goal as normal, not to double up on missed volume, which raises the risk of soreness or injury. Consistency over the 30 days matters more than any single streak.

## Keeping the streak going
Setting a daily reminder at the same time each day, and doing the first set right away when it goes off, is the simplest way to protect a streak. On a busy day, a few small sets spread across the day still count.

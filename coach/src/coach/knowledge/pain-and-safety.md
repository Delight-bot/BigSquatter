# Pain and safety

## Pain is different from effort
Burning in the thighs and heavy breathing are normal effort. Sharp pain, pain in a joint (knee, hip or lower back), or pain that changes how you move are signs to stop the set.

## What to do about pain
Stop for the day if a movement causes sharp or joint pain. If pain lasts more than a few days, keeps returning, or comes with swelling, see a doctor or physiotherapist before continuing. The coach cannot diagnose injuries.

## Reducing load safely
Squatting to a chair or box, reducing depth to a pain-free range, or lowering the daily number are all reasonable ways to keep going while a minor ache settles.

# Squat technique

## Stance
Stand with feet about shoulder-width apart, toes turned out slightly. Weight stays spread across the whole foot, with heels down throughout the rep.

## Knees
Knees should track in line with the toes. Knees caving inwards on the way up is common when fatigued; cue "push the floor apart" with the feet. Knees travelling forward past the toes is normal for many people and not a problem by itself if heels stay down and it feels comfortable.

## Back and breathing
Keep a neutral spine and the chest facing forward rather than the floor. Breathe in on the way down and out on the way up. Don't hold your breath for a whole set.

## Tempo
A controlled rep takes about two seconds down and one second up. The app ignores reps that are too fast to judge, so bouncing out of the bottom can make the count lower than the reps you actually did.
